### Why an ensemble

Single release models of the four configurations tied on cross-validation called 2.9–10.9 % of the 28,764 non-coding pool variants pathogenic; 5-seed ensembles of the same configurations called 2.5–3.7 % (the abstract model: 0.7 %). The configuration chosen by cross-validation was kept; the release procedure changed from one model to an ensemble.
